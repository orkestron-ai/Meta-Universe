## Meta‑Universe Version 1.1

# Status of This Document

This document defines the conceptual architecture, responsibilities, governance principles, lifecycle rules, federation behavior, and operational semantics of Dimensions and Galaxies inside Meta‑Universe version 1.1.

This document is part of the Meta‑Universe specification family and should be interpreted together with:
- Meta-Universe.md
- Meta-Entities.md
- Object.md
- Projection.md

This document standardizes:
- Dimension responsibilities,
- Galaxy structure,
- semantic governance,
- federation behavior,

- synchronization responsibilities,
- interoperability behavior.

# 1. Introduction

Meta‑Universe is a federated semantic interoperability environment.

Inside Meta‑Universe:
- Dimensions provide semantic authority and governance,
- Galaxies provide bounded semantic ecosystems.

Dimensions and Galaxies together form:
# the operational semantic governance layer of Meta‑Universe.

---

# 2. Dimension

# 2.1. Definition

Dimension is:
# an independent semantic registration and governance authority.

Dimension acts as:
- a semantic authority,
- a governance provider,
- a semantic standards provider,
- a trust domain,
- a federation participant.

Dimension defines:
- how semantic entities are registered,
- how ownership is verified,
- how federation works,
- how AI systems operate,
- how synchronization occurs,
- how semantic policies are enforced.

---

# 2.2. Why Dimensions Exist

Different ecosystems require:
- different governance,
- different security restrictions,
- different AI policies,
- different synchronization models,
- different semantic standards,
- different trust rules.

Because of this:
# Meta‑Universe is intentionally federated through Dimensions.

Dimensions allow:
- semantic autonomy,
- organizational isolation,
- independent semantic evolution,
- local governance,
- policy specialization.

# 2.6. Registration Models

Dimensions MAY implement different registration models.

---

## Open Registration

Any compatible entity MAY self-register.

Used for:
- public ecosystems,
- open-source environments,
- experimental systems.

---

## Verified Registration

Registration requires ownership verification.

Used for:
- enterprise systems,
- production ecosystems,
- regulated environments.

---

## Delegated Registration

Registration MAY occur through authorized representatives.

Used for:
- enterprise ecosystems,
- managed platforms,
- delegated governance systems.

---

## Centralized Registration

Registration occurs only through approved authorities.

Used for:
- military systems,
- government systems,
- highly restricted ecosystems.
